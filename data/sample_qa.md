**Q2: What is the lock-in period for HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund?**

> HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund has a statutory lock-in period of 3 years from the date of each SIP installment allotment.
> Redemption is not permitted before 3 years. This is the shortest lock-in among 80C tax-saving options.
>
> Source: https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-elss-tax-saver-fund-direct-plan-growth
> Last updated from sources: 2026-09-03
